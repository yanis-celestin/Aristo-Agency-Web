Designing e-detailing for busy healthcare professionals

A healthcare professional may give a representative only a few minutes. Interactive sales aids have to earn attention quickly and make every screen count.

Good e-detailing is less about how much information fits on a screen and more about how quickly the right information can be found. The best presentations feel like a guided conversation rather than a slide deck.

Lead with the essential message

Put the key takeaway first. Supporting data, references and detail should be one tap away for those who want them — not in the way for those who do not.

Let the conversation choose the path

Non-linear navigation lets the representative follow the professional's interests: jump to efficacy, to safety, or to a specific patient profile without scrolling through everything in between.

Design for the conversation you want to have, not for the deck you happen to have.

Clarity over decoration

  • One idea per screen, with a clear visual hierarchy.
  • Charts simplified to the point they make.
  • Consistent interactions, so nothing needs explaining.

When the design gets out of the way, the science can do the talking.
